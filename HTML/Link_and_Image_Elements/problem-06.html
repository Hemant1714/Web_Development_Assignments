TechFest 2026

Innovate. Create. Compete.

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is the biggest technology festival of the year! This fictional college technology festival brings together students who are interested in programming, artificial intelligence, robotics, innovation and emerging technologies. The event is designed to provide an exciting platform where students can learn, compete and share their ideas.

TechFest 2026 will encourage students to explore their creativity through technical competitions, demonstrations and interactive activities. Participants will have opportunities to work with other students, solve challenging problems and gain practical experience outside the classroom.

Highlights

The festival will include coding challenges, robotics demonstrations, AI-based projects, gaming competitions and technology exhibitions. Students can participate in different activities according to their interests and skills.

A special project showcase will allow students to present innovative ideas and working models. The event will also include interactive sessions where participants can learn about modern technologies and discuss their ideas with other students.

Date and Venue

Date: 15 October 2026
Time: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Venue: College Auditorium and Technology Block
Location: GLA University, Noida

Participants are encouraged to arrive early so that they can complete registration and prepare for their selected activities before the competitions begin.

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will feature fictional technology experts, developers and industry professionals who will share their experiences with students. The speakers will discuss topics such as artificial intelligence, software development, innovation and future career opportunities in technology.

The speaker sessions will give students an opportunity to understand how classroom knowledge can be applied to real-world technology projects. Students can also ask questions and learn from the experiences of professionals.

Registration

Students interested in participating should complete the registration process before the deadline. Registration Deadline: 5 October 2026.

Participants should provide accurate information while registering and carefully read the event instructions before submitting the registration form.
