Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair connects students, graduates and job seekers with companies, universities and training organizations.

Free registration is available for students and job seekers. The event focuses on career opportunities, internships and education.

Objectives: Career guidance, networking, skill development and employment.

"Our goal is to help every participant discover the right path for their future." — Event Organizer

Preparation Guide

  • Carry an updated resume.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Dress professionally.
  • Carry student or professional ID.
  1. Register for the event.
  2. Reach the venue on time.
  3. Attend your preferred sessions.
  4. Meet organizations and recruiters.
  5. Follow up after the event.

Important Notice

Arrive 30 minutes early for registration.

Keep digital and printed copies of your resume.
