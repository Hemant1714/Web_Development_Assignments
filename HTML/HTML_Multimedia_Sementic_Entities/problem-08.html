Personal Finance Made Simple

Start With the Basics

Personal finance is the way you earn, save, spend, borrow and invest money. A simple plan can help you manage monthly expenses, prepare for emergencies & work toward important goals.

Remember: saving even a small amount regularly can make a meaningful difference over time. Consistency > perfection.

Choose Financial Products Carefully

Bank accounts, cards and loans can be useful tools when selected for the right purpose. Always compare charges, rates, eligibility criteria & repayment terms before applying.

“Do not save what is left after spending; spend what is left after saving.”

— Warren Buffett

Education notice: Financial products involve terms, conditions and risks. Read all documents carefully before making a decision.

Accounts, Cards & Loans

A savings account helps you store and access money. A credit card allows short-term borrowing, while a loan is a larger amount repaid over an agreed period.

Use credit responsibly: pay bills on time, avoid unnecessary debt & keep your personal banking details private.

Responsible Personal-Finance Tips

  • Create a monthly budget for income, spending & savings.
  • Build an emergency fund for unexpected expenses.
  • Pay loan EMIs and card bills before their due dates.
  • Check interest rates, fees & penalties before borrowing.
  • Never share your OTP, PIN, password or card CVV.
  • Review bank statements regularly for unfamiliar transactions.
  • Set financial goals for education, travel, housing or retirement.
  • Seek qualified advice before making major investment decisions.

How to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose an account suitable for your needs.
  2. Check eligibility, required documents and minimum balance rules.
  3. Complete the application form online or at a branch.
  4. Submit identity and address verification documents.
  5. Receive confirmation, account details and banking access.

Useful Finance Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on borrowed money or earned on certain deposits, usually stated per year.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment — a fixed monthly payment used to repay a loan over time.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested, excluding interest.
Tenure
The agreed time period for a loan, deposit or financial arrangement.

Simple interest formula: Interest = Principal × Rate × Time. For example, ₹10,000 × 5% × 1 year = ₹500.
