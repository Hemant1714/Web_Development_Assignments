Destination Overview

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is famous for its grand forts, colourful markets, palaces and welcoming hospitality. Known as the Pink City, it offers a memorable mix of history, food, art & adventure.

Why Visit Jaipur?

From sunrise views at Amber Fort to handcrafted treasures in local bazaars, Jaipur has something for every traveller. Enjoy royal palaces, traditional cuisine & unforgettable photo spots.

One-Day Jaipur Itinerary

  1. Start with breakfast near Hawa Mahal.
  2. Visit Amber Fort and enjoy the panoramic views.
  3. Explore City Palace and Jantar Mantar.
  4. Try traditional Rajasthani lunch.
  5. Shop for jewellery, textiles & crafts at Johari Bazaar.
  6. End the day with sunset at Nahargarh Fort.
