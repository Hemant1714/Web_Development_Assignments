About Us

Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters is a popular Indian coffee brand known for freshly roasted coffee and high-quality beans sourced from different coffee-growing regions of India. The brand focuses on bringing fresh, flavourful coffee to coffee lovers.

Blue Tokai offers a comfortable café experience where customers can enjoy freshly prepared coffee, food and desserts. It is also known for roasting coffee beans in small batches to maintain their freshness and flavour.

Signature Items

Freshly Brewed Coffee is one of the main attractions at Blue Tokai. Customers can enjoy different types of freshly prepared coffee made using carefully roasted coffee beans.

Blue Tokai also offers a variety of espresso-based drinks, including cappuccino, latte and Americano. Each drink is prepared to highlight the natural flavours of the coffee beans.

Customers can also enjoy snacks and desserts along with their coffee. Freshly baked treats make a great combination with a hot cup of coffee.
