Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must carry their college ID card while attending classes and college activities.

2. Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories, and other college areas.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for semester examinations.

Students should attend classes regularly and inform the concerned faculty member in case of a genuine absence. Regular attendance helps students understand the course better.

Examination

4. Students must carry their valid ID card during examinations.
Electronic devices such as mobile phones are not allowed inside the examination hall.

Students arriving late by more than 30 minutes may not be allowed to enter the examination hall.

Assignment Submission

5. All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

Assignments submitted after the deadline may receive a penalty of marks.
Students should follow the submission instructions provided by the faculty.

Students are advised to keep a backup of their assignments before submission.

Discipline

6. Students must behave respectfully with teachers, staff, and other students.

7. Bullying, fighting, cheating, and damaging college property are strictly prohibited.

Violation of college rules may result in disciplinary action.

Important Notice

All students are responsible for reading and following these rules. Rules may be updated by the institution when necessary.
