About University

GLA University is an educational institution that provides students with opportunities to develop academic knowledge, technical skills, and professional abilities. The university focuses on creating a learning environment where students can explore their interests and prepare themselves for their future careers.

The university encourages students to participate in academic activities, practical projects, competitions, and other skill-development programs. Students are also encouraged to develop good communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Education is not only about scoring marks; it is also about gaining knowledge, developing confidence, and becoming prepared for real-world challenges.

Departments

The university has different academic departments that offer specialized education in various fields. These departments provide students with theoretical knowledge as well as practical learning opportunities.

The Computer Science and Engineering department focuses on programming, software development, databases, computer networks, and modern technologies.

The Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning field focuses on intelligent systems, data analysis, machine learning algorithms, and applications of AI in different industries.

Other areas of study include engineering, management, business, and other professional programs that help students build their academic and career interests.

Academic Programs

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in different areas. Students can choose a program according to their interests, career goals, and academic background.

A B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning provides students with knowledge of programming, mathematics, data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

Campus Facilities

The university provides various facilities to support students in their academic and personal development. Classrooms and laboratories provide an environment for regular learning and practical work.

Students can also make use of library resources for reading and research. Computer laboratories provide access to systems and software required for programming and technical subjects.

Sports and recreational facilities encourage students to maintain a balance between academics and physical activities. Such facilities can help students improve teamwork, discipline, and leadership skills.

Students are expected to use all campus facilities responsibly and maintain cleanliness and discipline.

Important Information

Students should regularly attend their classes and complete their academic work on time. Maintaining the required attendance percentage is important for academic eligibility.

Assignments, projects, and practical work should be submitted according to the deadlines provided by the respective faculty members.

Students should check important academic announcements and examination schedules regularly.

Students should also follow the university's rules regarding discipline, examinations, attendance, and the responsible use of campus resources.
