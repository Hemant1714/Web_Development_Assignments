India Announces Major Push for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation

The Government of India has announced a new initiative to promote Artificial Intelligence, digital technology, and innovation across the country. The initiative aims to encourage students, startups, researchers, and technology companies to develop innovative solutions for real-world problems.

The announcement was made in New Delhi during a technology and innovation event attended by government officials, industry leaders, researchers, and students. The programme will focus on improving access to modern technology and providing better opportunities for young people interested in computer science and artificial intelligence.

According to officials, special attention will be given to education, skill development, research, and employment opportunities. Several institutions are expected to introduce new training programmes to help students learn programming, machine learning, data science, and other emerging technologies. This initiative could play an important role in preparing young people for the jobs of the future.

The programme is expected to begin on 1 September 2026, with different phases being introduced across various cities. Officials said that students and young professionals will be encouraged to participate in technology-based projects and competitions.

Sports News: Young Players Prepare for Major Cricket Tournament

Young cricket players from different parts of the country are preparing for an upcoming national-level tournament. The competition will provide talented players with an opportunity to demonstrate their skills and gain valuable experience.

The tournament will be held in October 2026 and is expected to attract a large number of cricket fans. Coaches are currently conducting training sessions focusing on fitness, batting, bowling, and fielding.

Organisers said that the tournament will help identify talented players who could represent their teams at higher levels in the future. The main objective is to provide young cricketers with a professional platform to showcase their talent.
